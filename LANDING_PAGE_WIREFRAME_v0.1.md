# Active Wellness Stack Landing Page Wireframe v0.1

Purpose: review the page story, content, quiz/questionnaire flow, and missing visuals before revising the live full-stack page.

Status: Draft for founder review.

## Strategic Direction

The landing page should introduce the idea before the final brand name.

Public-facing framing:

> A daily wellness stack for active Indonesians.

Internal brand architecture:

> Move. Focus. Recover.

First product validation:

> Creatine-based Move stack, with two MVP format paths: drink mix / stick pack and gummy.

Primary CTA:

> Take the 2-minute product quiz

Primary business goal:

> Collect early customer feedback, product-format signals, trust barriers, and sample-testing leads.

### 5. Creatine Education

Goal: answer “why creatine?” without sounding medical or gym-bro.

Headline:

> Why start with creatine?

Core message:

> Creatine is one of the most researched ingredients in sports nutrition, but many people still see it as complicated, intimidating, or only for bodybuilders.

Education blocks:

1. What creatine is
   - A compound involved in cellular energy recycling.
   - Commonly discussed in strength, training, repeated effort, and active living contexts.

2. Why people use it
   - To support training routines and repeated high-demand movement.
   - To support consistency in active lifestyles.
   - To make supplement routines simpler when the format is easy.

3. Why it is misunderstood
   - “Will I get bulky?”
   - “Will I get bloated?”
   - “Is it only for men?”
   - “Is gummy just candy?”
   - “Is the dose credible?”

4. What we will not claim
   - No instant transformation.
   - No fat-loss promise.
   - No medical or disease claims.
   - No BPOM/Halal certification claims before approval.

Research proof style:

- Use “research-backed context,” not “clinically proven results.”
- Link to a compact research library or expandable cards.
- Use 5-7 safe research categories:
  - strength and lean mass context
  - repeated effort / sprint context
  - cognition / mental fatigue context, carefully worded
  - women and active health context
  - healthy aging / muscle context
  - safety and common misconceptions

Visual:

- Research cards with short human translations.
- Avoid dense citation wall on first page.
- Add “View research notes” expandable section.

Headline:

Position:

Needs validation:

Position:

Needs validation:

- adult texture
- sweetness
- sugar concern
- dose credibility
- candy perception
- 28-day repeatability

Visuals needed:

- Drink mix stick pack mockup

### 8. Interactive Quiz / Questionnaire

Goal: collect feedback without feeling like a boring form.

Recommended structure:

- 7 short interactive steps
- Mostly tap-based choices
- 1 optional open text field
- Progress bar
- Preview insight before email capture
- Email / WhatsApp at the end

Flow:

1. Your active profile
   - Gym / strength
   - Running / endurance
   - Padel / racket sports
   - Busy professional
   - Active parent
   - Wellness beginner
   - Pilates / yoga / boutique fitness

2. Current supplement behavior
   - I already take creatine
   - I take supplements, but not creatine
   - I am curious but unsure
   - I rarely take supplements

3. Creatine perception
   - Good for strength
   - Only for bodybuilders
   - Useful but confusing
   - I worry about side effects
   - I do not know enough yet

4. Biggest barrier
   - Taste
   - Price
   - Sugar
   - Bloating
   - Bulky body image
   - Halal / BPOM trust
   - Dose credibility
   - Remembering to take it

5. Preferred first format
   - Drink mix / stick pack
   - Gummy
   - Capsule
   - I do not care, as long as it works

6. Purchase and sample signal
   - I would try a free sample
   - I would buy if the taste is good
   - I would buy if BPOM/Halal is clear
   - I need more education first
   - I am just curious for now

7. Contact capture
   - first name
   - email
   - WhatsApp
   - optional note

Output after submit:

> Thanks. Based on your answers, you look like a [profile type]. We will use your feedback to shape the first Move product.

Possible profile types:

- Active Professional
- Consistency Seeker
- Weekend Athlete
- Strength Beginner
- Research-First Buyer
- Active Wellness Explorer

Important:

- This should be a product validation quiz, not a medical recommendation quiz.
- Keep research-backed personalization, but do not overclaim.
- We can reuse logic and tone from the existing creatine quiz project, simplified for speed.
